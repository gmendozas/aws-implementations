A Quiet Hour in the City

At dawn, the city belongs to its early risers. A baker lifts the shutters of a corner shop, a cyclist crosses an empty avenue, and the first bus traces a bright line through the mist.

By midday, those quiet streets have become a chorus of footsteps, conversations, and distant music. Each person is carrying a small story: a new idea, a familiar errand, or the promise of meeting someone they have missed.

When evening arrives, windows glow above the sidewalks. The city slows, but it never quite falls silent. Somewhere, a kettle begins to sing; somewhere else, a reader turns one last page. In these ordinary moments, a busy place makes room for rest.
